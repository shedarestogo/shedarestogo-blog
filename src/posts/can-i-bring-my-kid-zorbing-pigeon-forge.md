Zorbing is exactly what it sounds like. You climb inside a giant clear inflatable ball and roll down the side of a hill. That is the whole activity, and it is as fun as it sounds. If you have never heard of it, you are not alone. I had not either until my daughter found a video and would not stop talking about it.

My reel about it started with this hook: "Why would you take a 6 hour road trip from Virginia to roll down a hill in Tennessee?" The answer is simple. My daughter wanted to try it, and she is almost 9, which is the perfect age for big, silly, unforgettable adventures.

## The verdict: yes, bring the kid

She loved every second of it. I loved watching her love it. And I have zero regrets about the drive. Some trips are worth it for one perfect hour, and this was one of them. The drive is part of the story too. Six hours each way sounds like a lot until you are singing along with your kid somewhere in the Tennessee mountains and it feels like the best decision you ever made.

## 3 quick tips before you go

**1. Check the rules online first.** Every operator posts their age, height, and size requirements on their website. Read them before you drive out so there are no surprises at the gate.

**2. Go early in the day.** Mornings are calmer, lines are shorter, and your kid will have more energy for the drive home after.

**3. Pack a change of clothes.** Between the ride and running around after, your kid will be a mess in the best way. Throw an extra outfit and some snacks in the car.

That is the whole review. One wild ride, one happy kid, one very worth it road trip.
